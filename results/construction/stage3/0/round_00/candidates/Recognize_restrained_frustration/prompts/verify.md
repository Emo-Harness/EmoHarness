1. Decide what the heard cues on their own point to, ignoring the earlier ranking.
2. Decide what the seen cues on their own point to.

Frustration calibration:

- When `Frustration` is a candidate, count blocked goals, difficulty, complaints, impatience, irritation, repeated questions, demanding language, helpless inability, and problem-solving around an obstacle as diagnostic evidence.

# Strict review of the plan, 2026-09-07

Lens: a skeptical reviewer at a top ML or multimedia venue reading the plan as if it were the paper's method and experiment sections. Each finding lists the severity, the problem, and what changed in the plan.

## Critical

1. **H3 was already satisfied by the baseline.** Qwen2.5-VL-7B retains 83% of Gemini-2.5-Pro's CoT score with no harness at all. A success criterion the baseline meets is not a hypothesis. Changed: H3 is now Gap Closed on Recognition with pre-registered tiers (ADR-0012); retention is descriptive and shown next to the baseline value.
2. **The main comparison confounded "adding an audio tool" with "the Builder built something".** The harness gives the Target a modality it lacks; Audio-Reasoner shows audio alone moves Recognition by about ten points. Changed: the ladder L0–L6 (ADR-0009) with L1 (Direct + audio text) and L3 (human scaffold with the same tools); the central claim is Builder gain L5 − L3, measured on all 6,500 items.
3. **Held-out contamination through construction media.** FG-SA uses MOSI/MOSEI-style monologues and IR likely uses TV dialogue clips; both are held-out, and MELD and MOSEI are construction sources. Id matching alone would miss re-encoded clips. Changed: perceptual-hash and transcript-similarity checks against all eight tasks are mandatory gates (ADR-0003).
4. **Held-out knowledge could enter through the human-written controller.** The first draft branched on task types and set a cardinality cap after looking at ML-ER label counts. Changed: the controller parses the public prompt grammar only; the cap is a disclosed prior with a sensitivity analysis; ADR-0001 now states exactly what "held-out" excludes and what it cannot exclude.
5. **The failure taxonomy names held-out task types.** Giving it to the Builder would inject multi-label, fine-grained, and intent knowledge. Changed: the taxonomy is hidden from the Builder and used only after the freeze (ADR-0005).

## High

6. **The Reasoning score can be raised by output format alone.** Changed: L2 is the format-only control; every hypothesis uses Recognition as the primary metric.
7. **Judge cost was underestimated by about two times.** GPT-4o charges per image tile; ten frames of 720p video cost about 11k tokens. Changed: cost model and cut line in ADR-0009; Gemini Direct moves to the subset (ADR-0008); the L3 full run is protected because it carries the central claim.
8. **Thirty-two selection decisions on one validation set invite overfitting.** Changed: D_ext-test (300 items) evaluated once per frozen stage (ADR-0003).
9. **One construction run cannot show that the procedure is stable.** Changed: three Stage 2 replicates; the first completed run proceeds, fixed in advance (ADR-0006).
10. **No pre-registration and no written firewall.** Changed: ADR-0012 with tags, hashes, inspection rules, post-freeze patch logging, and a negative-results policy.
11. **The subset was stratified by label with equal allocation**, which would bias subset scores relative to the full set. Changed: proportional stratification (ADR-0013).
12. **No statement of detectable effect sizes.** Changed: ADR-0013 gives them per item set; per-task subset numbers are descriptive only.
13. **H4 was claimed on one Qwen-family point.** Changed: H4 is exploratory; a second family is P2.

## Medium

14. **Audio pipeline banned all emotion words**, discarding the audio-model judgment that audio-native competitors use. Changed: two channels, cue and opinion, with the opinion ablated (ADR-0004).
15. **Nothing handled clips without speech or with several speakers, or recorded language.** Changed: ADR-0004.
16. **Without a call cache, each candidate re-runs every stage.** Changed: content-addressed cache keyed on prompt text and inputs (ADR-0006), which also guarantees identical inputs across methods.
17. **Judge noise was never measured.** Changed: official judge test-retest on 300 items (ADR-0007).
18. **The judge reads a Qwen2-Audio clue while the harness reasons from a different audio description.** Changed: an appendix analysis re-judges 300 items with the harness's audio text (ADR-0007).
19. **Retries were not counted in the call budget.** Changed: one retry per stage inside the six-call cap (ADR-0011).
20. **The Stage 1 acceptance criterion "beats Direct" would tempt human tuning.** Changed: acceptance is end-to-end validity and inspectability (plan M4).
21. **Benchmark Direct runs were scheduled before the freeze.** Changed: only the Qwen2.5-VL-7B calibration runs before the freeze, with outputs uninspected (ADR-0012, plan M1 and M7).
22. **No related-work positioning against automated prompt and pipeline optimizers.** Changed: `docs/paper-outline.md`; an established optimizer is a P2 row or a stated limitation.
23. **Official judge model versions may be retired by the time of the runs.** Changed: availability check in M1 with a recorded offset if a replacement is needed (plan).
24. **The FG-SA judge prompt contains contradictory examples** ("positive" versus "strong positive" rated 0/1 in one example and 1/1 in the next). Recorded in `docs/benchmark-facts.md` and reported as a benchmark note; our exact-string answers are unaffected.

## Verified during the review

- FG-ER's judge also scores the last step C/K; its own example rewards seven predicted labels against one gold label with 1/1.
- The judge sees frames at 1 fps capped at ten, so only the first ten seconds of a clip.
- The dataset zips contain small clips (IEMOCAP clips 60–430 KB), so judge cost varies by task; the estimate must be measured, not assumed.

## Residual risks the plan cannot remove

- Authors know the public task list; the paper says so rather than pretending otherwise.
- One benchmark, one primary Target, one Builder.
- The Gemini upper bound is estimated on 1,000 items unless budget remains.
